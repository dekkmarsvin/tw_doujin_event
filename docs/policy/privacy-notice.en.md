## 1. Scope and operator

This notice applies to the official website above and the web pages, interfaces and related features it provides, and explains how personal data involved in the service is collected, processed and used.

The data collector and service operator is the **tw_doujin_event project operator** (“this site”). Other services set up or derived from this project’s source code are the responsibility of whoever sets up or operates each of them, and this notice does not apply to them.

## 2. General browsing, browser storage and sharing features

Browsing public maps, events and catalog content does not require an account.

This site may use cookies, browser storage, offline caches or similar technologies to maintain sign-in state, remember interface preferences, or store personal data such as favorites, groups and plans. Data stored only in your browser may not be recoverable after you clear browser data or switch devices; if you actively use sharing links, cross-device sync or other server-assisted features, the necessary data will be transmitted to and stored in this site’s systems.

## 3. Categories of data collected

Depending on the features used and how you interact with the service, this site may collect or process the following categories of data (the actual scope depends on the features you use):

- **Account, contact and settings data**: for example, email addresses, sign-in and verification information, interface and notification preferences, and account, administration or collaboration permission settings.
- **Application, verification and correspondence records**: for example, URLs, explanations of relationship and necessary proof submitted when claiming a circle or applying for an event or workspace, review results, and information provided when contacting this site by email or other channels.
- **Content provided or shared by users**: for example, circle names, pen names, works and item list information, sales details, event announcements, links, images, tags, shared plan content, and other data you actively submit, upload or choose to make public.
- **Event, venue and map data**: for example, event dates, venues, booth layouts, circle lists, map and layout drafts, submitted content, and necessary source and version records.
- **Notification and service interaction records**: for example, notification preferences, delivery status and necessary records, as well as data generated when using interactive, collaborative or personalized features.
- **Technical, security, activity and statistical data**: for example, sign-in status, connection and device technical information, security verification, records of actions such as claiming, editing, reviewing or deleting, and aggregated or statistical information used to maintain performance and analyze usage.

Circle, event, map and related content that is marked, designed or chosen by you to be public or shared will be displayed on the public website or accessible to others via sharing links; account, verification, security and internal activity data are, in principle, not made public. Public content and external links or images you include may be subject to the caches and policies of external platforms, search engines or third parties, which this site cannot fully control.

## 4. Purposes and how data is used

To the extent reasonably necessary to provide, maintain and develop the service, this site collects, processes and uses data for the following purposes:

- Providing, maintaining and operating the map, catalog, search, favorites, sharing, offline use and related features.
- Handling account sign-in, identity and permission verification, circle claims, event applications, reviews, and workspace and collaboration features.
- Displaying, storing, syncing and managing event, venue, booth, circle, announcement and map content provided by users, circles or organizers.
- Sending notifications and communications related to accounts, applications, reviews, permissions, content updates or operations.
- Maintaining system and data security, preventing abuse, troubleshooting, and handling anomalies or rights disputes.
- Conducting usage analysis, aggregated statistics, service improvement, development of new features, and fulfilling necessary legal or regulatory requirements.

Data is used as follows:

- **Period of use**: for as long as the collection purposes above remain in effect, while the account or service relationship exists, and for as long as necessary under applicable law or for service security, protection of rights or dispute handling; when there is no longer a need to retain it, this site may delete, cease using or de-identify the data according to its nature and technical conditions.
- **Region of use**: Taiwan, and the regions where this site and its cloud, hosting, communications, security or related service providers operate.
- **Who uses the data**: this site’s operator, event organizers, collaborators or administrators authorized for a feature, third-party providers supplying website hosting, data storage, email delivery, security, analytics and other operationally necessary services, and parties to whom disclosure is required by law or competent authorities. Public or shared content may be viewed by any visitor or anyone who obtains the link.
- **How data is used**: data is collected, processed, used and internationally transmitted through automated machines, information systems, web interfaces, email or other non-automated means (including necessary manual review and contact); this site may also de-identify or aggregate data into statistical information for service maintenance, analysis and improvement.

## 5. Effect of not providing data

General public browsing does not require providing account data.

If you choose not to provide necessary data required for a specific feature (such as the email address needed for sign-in verification, or required fields for claiming, applying, contributing, organizing, notifications or sharing), you may be unable to use all or part of that feature. Providing optional public circle information or preference settings is up to you.

## 6. Your personal data rights

Under applicable law, you may make the following requests regarding personal data held by this site:

- To inquire about or review it.
- To receive a copy of it.
- To supplement or correct it.
- To stop its collection, processing or use.
- To delete it.

Some content you maintain yourself can be edited or deleted directly in the control panel; for other personal data requests, please email `maintain@kotoban.top`. To prevent unauthorized changes or deletion, this site may verify the requester’s identity or permissions before processing and will handle requests within a reasonable period under applicable law.

Where required by law, necessary for service operation and security maintenance (including necessary security, anti-abuse or activity records), involved in handling rights disputes, or where data has been de-identified so that it can no longer identify a specific individual, this site may retain necessary records or limit deletion and cessation of use. Deleting an account or ceasing use does not affect previously published event, venue, map or collaborative records, nor does it extend to content already cached or copied by external services or third parties.

If newly added data processing legally requires separate notice or consent, it will be handled within the relevant feature or process.
